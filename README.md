# Yehuda Landau

**PhD Candidate in Biomedical Engineering | Tel Aviv University**

[Laboratory of Computational Systems and Synthetic Biology](https://www.cs.tau.ac.il/~tamirtul/) · Prof. Tamir Tuller

My research lies at the intersection of **synthetic biology, computational biology, and molecular biophysics**. I develop computational and theoretical approaches to understand how biomolecular sequence, structure, and dynamics determine molecular function and gene regulation, and to translate these insights into the rational design of biological systems.

My work combines mechanistic modeling, algorithm development, and machine learning, in collaboration with experimental researchers.

### Research Interests

- **Synthetic Biology & RNA Engineering** — Computational and inverse design of programmable RNA regulators, toehold switches, and RNA-based regulatory systems.

- **RNA Folding & Molecular Biophysics** — Statistical-mechanical and kinetic modeling of RNA folding, with a focus on free-energy landscapes, conformational entropy, and molecular simulations.

- **Computational Structural Biology** — Structure-informed modeling and design of RNA, proteins, and their molecular interactions, integrating physics-based and machine-learning approaches.

- **Gene Expression & Sequence Engineering** — Predictive modeling and optimization of coding and regulatory sequences to control transcription and translation, including codon optimization.

My collaborative research also explores applications in infectious diseases, cancer, neurodegenerative disorders, and environmental biotechnology.
